# Data and business definitions

- Original notebook citation: Cameron Seamons, [Customer purchase behavior — Electronic Sales Data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/cameronseamons/electronic-sales-sep2023-sep2024), September 2024.
- The original bundled CSV is retained unchanged. Its SHA-256, row counts and coverage dates are in reports/run.json.
- Currency is shown with $ to follow the original project; no exchange-rate or currency conversion is applied.
- Total Price excludes Add-on Total in all displayed merchandise-revenue measures. Completed status is a proxy for realized revenue, not verified cash settlement.
- No observed retention or marketing uplift is available.

Technical references: [Prophet](https://facebook.github.io/prophet/), [SHAP](https://shap.readthedocs.io/en/latest/), [LIME](https://github.com/marcotcr/lime), [Streamlit](https://docs.streamlit.io/).
